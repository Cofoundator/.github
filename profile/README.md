### Cofoundator

**Building the infrastructure for autonomous digital workers.**

We are building a new generation of software infrastructure where AI systems can operate as persistent, specialized digital workers.

### Our Focus
We build the foundations required to make autonomous work reliable:
*   **Identity** — persistent identities for autonomous workers.
*   **Execution** — systems that turn missions into autonomous action.
*   **Memory** — continuity, experience and accumulated knowledge.
*   **Trust** — permissions, verification and accountability.
*   **Infrastructure** — the runtime and environments that make autonomous systems operational.

### Our Work
We are currently developing **Employee**, a platform where companies can recruit, manage, and evaluate autonomous digital workers with cryptographically verifiable identities.
